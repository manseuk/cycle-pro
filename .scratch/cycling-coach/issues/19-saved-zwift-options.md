# 19: Saved Zwift options

**What to build:** A Cyclist can manually save a Zwift event, race, or route, associate it with the Primary active goal, and review it on the calendar with a link to Zwift.

**Blocked by:** 14: Training goal management; 16: Ride calendar and time-zone display.

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] A Cyclist can save a Zwift event or race with name, date/time, route, URL, and optional notes.
- [ ] A Cyclist can save a Zwift route with name, date/time, route, URL, and optional notes.
- [ ] A saved option can be associated with the Primary active goal.
- [ ] Saved options appear on the appropriate calendar date and are visually distinct from completed Rides and accepted workouts.
- [ ] Opening a saved option follows its supplied Zwift link.
- [ ] Zwift listings are not scraped or automatically collected, and the app does not require Zwift credentials or account synchronization.
- [ ] Saved options are private to the owning Cyclist.

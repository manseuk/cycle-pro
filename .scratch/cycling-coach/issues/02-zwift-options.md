## Question

What first-party Zwift interfaces, feeds, terms, or documented integration paths are available for discovering and displaying selected routes, races, and rides in a separate training app? Identify practical data access limits and what a read-only display with links can support. Use official sources and separate documented facts from inference.

## Answer

Zwift provides official events and route pages and selected partner integrations, but public documentation reviewed here does not expose a general event, route, or personal ride-history API. Its terms restrict scraping and unauthorized app interactions. The low-dependency MVP path is user-entered/saved options and links to official Zwift pages; pursue partner access before promising automated listings or account sync. See the [Zwift research report](../research/zwift-options.md) for source citations and limits.

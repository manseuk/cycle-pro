# 16: Ride calendar and time-zone display

**What to build:** A Cyclist can browse imported Rides on a month calendar or small-screen list, see them grouped by local calendar date, and open each Ride's details.

**Blocked by:** 15: FIT import and Ride management.

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] The calendar has a month view and a list view suited to smaller screens.
- [ ] Imported Rides appear on the correct date in the Cyclist's selected calendar time zone.
- [ ] Selecting a date shows its Ride items, and selecting a Ride opens its details.
- [ ] Ride items are clearly distinguished as completed activity.
- [ ] Calendar access and Ride details are restricted to the owning Cyclist.
- [ ] Deleting a Ride removes it from calendar views.

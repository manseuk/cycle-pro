## Question

What does “show selected Zwift training options” mean in the MVP? Decide which Zwift items are in scope, how they are selected and refreshed, what details the app shows, and how a user moves from a suggestion to Zwift. Use the integration research to set feasible boundaries; account sync remains out of scope.

## Comments

The user agreed that the MVP should let a cyclist save Zwift events/races and routes manually, with name, date/time, route, URL, and optional notes, and associate the saved option with the active goal. The app links out to Zwift for details and participation; it does not automatically collect event or route listings. Tighter Zwift integration may be considered later.

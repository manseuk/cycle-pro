## Question

What does the referenced WKO-Q material define about cycling fitness and readiness, and what inputs, formulas, or caveats does it identify? Use the first-party article and other primary sources where relevant. Separate source facts from interpretation, and identify what would need validation before using the model to make training suggestions.

## Answer

WKO-Q describes TSS, CTL (42-day EWMA), ATL (7-day EWMA), and TSB (yesterday's CTL minus ATL). These are relative training-load/form indicators, not standalone performance or readiness predictors. Power-based load needs duration, usable power, and a valid FTP; missing power, initialization, and historical data need explicit handling before making suggestions. See the [WKO-Q research report](../research/wko-q.md) for source citations and validation caveats.
